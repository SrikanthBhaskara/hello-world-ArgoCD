# ArgoCD / Kubernetes Interview Questions: Beginner to 6 Years

## Purpose

This note set organizes ArgoCD and Kubernetes interview preparation by experience level using the repo's project docs, ArgoCD manifests, and deployment notes as the source baseline.

## Files

- `argocd-kubernetes-interview-questions-beginner-0-to-2-years.md`
- `argocd-kubernetes-interview-questions-intermediate-2-to-4-years.md`
- `argocd-kubernetes-interview-questions-experienced-4-to-6-years.md`

## Recommended Study Order

1. Kubernetes basics: pods, deployments, services, namespaces, ConfigMaps, Secrets
2. Operational basics: `kubectl`, logs, describe, events, rollout, restart, probes
3. GitOps basics: ArgoCD Applications, sync, drift, declarative deployment, Helm and Kustomize
4. Platform depth: External Secrets, ECR auth, certificates, promotion flow, troubleshooting

## Interview Strategy

- Beginner interviews focus on Kubernetes object basics and simple `kubectl` troubleshooting.
- Intermediate interviews focus on manifests, Helm, ArgoCD sync behavior, secrets, and environment promotion.
- 4 to 6 year interviews focus on GitOps architecture, security, production troubleshooting, and platform tradeoffs.
